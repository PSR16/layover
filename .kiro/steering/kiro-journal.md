# Build journal

Keep `docs/kiro-log.md` current. It is evidence for the Kiroween 2026 judges.

- After finishing any spec task, hook, agent, MCP setup or significant change, add an entry under the matching section of `docs/kiro-log.md`. Do this before saying the task is done.
- Be factual and specific. Name files, requirement IDs, evidence IDs and test names. Do not exaggerate or record things that did not happen.
- Record the user's corrections and rejections in their own words, quoted briefly.
- When something would make a good screenshot or demo moment, add it to the "Screenshots to take" checklist.
- Never delete earlier entries. Mark corrections as dated "Update" lines.
- Keep the "Numbers" table in step with the work (tasks, tests, bugs, hours).
